GOLDEN RULE META-STRATEGY (Synthesized 2026-04-20):
The model's core failure is overvaluing static advantages (draw, jockey stats) as absolute guarantees, especially on a neutral "Good" track. The Golden Rule is: **Drastically penalize any highly-tipped horse where the predicted win relies on a specific, uncontested tactical scenario from its draw.** If the horse *needs* to lead from an inside gate or sit covered from a wide one to win, and the jockey's current form or the race's early pace pressure suggests this scenario is unlikely, the bet fails. On "Good" surfaces, treat all draw advantages as conditional and heavily downgrade runners dependent on perfect, jockey-executed trips.
